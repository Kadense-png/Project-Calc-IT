About Me

My name is Geremy Laurence D. Sanchez, I'm a second-year Web Development student who, if kept away from his video-games for too long, will probably go clinically insane. This portfolio of mine is evidence that, despite losing my mind, I didn't lose a single one of my Calculus papers and have the complete set of Artifacts. I chose IT-WebDev on a whim, I flipped a coin which made me choose between a set of options I've already forgotten. I've stayed in IT-WebDev because I found the ability to make sites like this to be helpful, and begrudgingly I found it fun, too. I might as well put it here, I honestly expected Calculus to be one of those subjects that just pissed me off, a non-major subject that would take up more of my time than majors, but for once in my life as a student, the major subjects this year took up all my time and the minor subjects didn't. Despite occupying much less time, Calculus still left me with a handful of takeaways, though I don't see myself using the formulas, the Calculus itself, I see myself using the concepts discussed and their ideas in everything else.
